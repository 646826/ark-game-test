# Game design

## Player promise

Restore an impossible clockwork greenhouse one satisfying circuit at a time. Every level is a compact spatial puzzle, every solved path creates a visible bloom, and every chapter repairs part of a larger living conservatory.

## Core loop

1. Read the powered turquoise flow from the Sunwell.
2. Rotate brass mechanisms clockwise.
3. Connect every potted specimen and close every powered leak.
4. Watch the full network ignite and all flowers bloom.
5. Earn score and stars, restore the next chamber, and expand the specimen collection.

## Modes

### Campaign

A guided progression through named conservatory chambers. The first three levels teach one concept each. Later levels use deterministic generated boards with increasing dimensions and occasional anchored mechanisms.

### Daily Bloom

One UTC seed per day, shared by every player. The mode supports best score, streak, and optional leaderboard posting.

### Zen Garden

An untimed, endlessly replayable variation for relaxed sessions.

## Progression

- five named chamber milestones;
- sixty-level restoration meter;
- one specimen initially available and four milestone unlocks;
- total score, best score, stars, and daily streak;
- visual theme changes every five levels.

## Scoring

Score rewards level complexity, move efficiency, and completion time. Hints reduce the final score. Stars summarize performance without blocking progression.

## Monetization boundaries

- interstitials only after completed campaign groups;
- three free hints per run;
- rewarded video only after free hints are exhausted and only after explicit player action;
- failed, missing, or cancelled rewarded video never grants a production reward;
- no ad interrupts an active puzzle.

## Visual direction

A premium adult-casual blend of Victorian conservatory architecture, precision brass mechanisms, translucent teal glass, deep emerald foliage, warm sunlight, and luminous aqua aetherlight. Gameplay clarity always outranks ornament: powered/unpowered, selected, fixed, plant, source, and leak states must remain distinguishable at mobile size.
