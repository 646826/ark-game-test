# Clockwork Conservatory — Arkadium One-Pager

## Hook

Restore miniature mechanical gardens by rotating brass circuits until aetherlight reaches every flower and no active leak remains.

## Why it fits

- One primary verb: rotate.
- Calm, untimed default play with optional move/time mastery.
- Adult-friendly botanical theme, high readability, no violence or controversial content.
- Three-to-eight-minute sessions, campaign restoration, deterministic Daily Bloom, Zen mode, stars, streaks, specimens, and leaderboard support.
- Mouse, touch, and keyboard; desktop, tablet, and portrait mobile.

## Differentiation

The familiar network-connection rule is presented as a premium living diorama. Energy visibly travels through brass and glass mechanisms, plants awaken, leaks erupt at exact endpoints, and a completed circuit restores the greenhouse. Dense mobile boards change camera composition rather than becoming illegible.

## Technology

- Standards-based TypeScript, Canvas 2D, DOM, and Web Audio.
- Approximately 0.30 MB to first interaction and 0.63 MB total in release candidate 0.3.0.
- Deterministic connected-tree generator and local hint planner; no runtime AI or backend dependency.
- Arkadium SDK v2 lifecycle, persistence, analytics, ads, leaderboards, pause/resume, and graceful standalone fallback.

## Monetization

- Interstitial only at completed campaign-group breaks.
- Three free hints per level.
- Additional hints through optional player-initiated rewarded video.
- Complete experience when ads, wallet, or leaderboard are unavailable.

## Current state

Production-oriented vertical slice with complete core loop, three onboarding levels, generated campaign, Daily and Zen modes, progress/meta, responsive art direction, five UI languages, automated generator tests, browser smoke tests, and SDK mock validation. Remaining work is publisher onboarding: Sandbox evidence, production IDs/configuration, native localization review, real-device matrix, endurance sign-off, and producer feedback.
