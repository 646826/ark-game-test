## [0.3.0] - 2026-08-04

### Added

- Premium greenhouse art direction and responsive 2.5D renderer.
- Dense portrait projection for readable large mobile boards.
- Campaign restoration menu, map, specimens, stars, streaks, Daily Bloom, and Zen Garden.
- Three guided onboarding levels, local Garden Hint, undo, resume, restart, view rotation, and synthesized audio.
- Arkadium SDK v2 lifecycle outbox, persistence, analytics, ads, leaderboard, and host pause/resume.
- English, Spanish, French, German, and Italian interface tables.
- Strict source checks, 234 generated-puzzle cases, browser smoke, mocked SDK lifecycle verification, CI, and Pages deployment.
- Asset provenance, publishing checklist, QA plan, pitch, and release documentation.

### Changed

- Replaced flat prototype rendering with layered brass, glass, energy, botanical, leak, particle, and victory states.
- Changed player-facing “AI Hint” terminology to “Garden Hint.”
- Made optional scene art lazy; only title art blocks first interaction.
- Made rewarded hints fail closed outside explicit local debug mode.
